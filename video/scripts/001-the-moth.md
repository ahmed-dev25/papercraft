# 001 - The Moth Who Refused the Lamp

**Logline.** A moth spends her wings on a light that never dims for her, and finds the moon at the
window the whole time.

**Metaphor.** Lamp = attention/validation that only takes. Moon = the steady thing that asks nothing.
Wings = what she spends. Window = the way out, always there.

**Set.** Lamp room at night. Back wall with a window and a shutter, a table, a shelf, a rug, a sill.
Lamp: a folded paper lantern, open like a flower. Moon: a pale card beyond the window.

**Cast.** The moth. No other characters.

**Runtime.** 88 s, 8 beats.

| # | Time | Shot | Text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:12 | establish | She had one rule: never fly toward the lamp. | Wide, still. The lamp is the brightest thing in the room. Dust in the warm pool of light. The shutter is closed. |
| 2 | 0:12-0:23 | approach | But it was the brightest thing in the room. | The moth enters from the window frame and begins to circle. Slow push-in. |
| 3 | 0:23-0:33 | closeup | Every circle cost her a little wing. | Close on the wings. A pale edge frays with each pass. The lamp does not react. |
| 4 | 0:33-0:44 | burn | The light did not dim for her. | A wing grazes the lantern. Three embers. The wing curls. The flame holds steady. |
| 5 | 0:44-0:55 | retreat | She had nothing left to give it. | She drops to the sill, folded small. The lamp keeps burning above her. |
| 6 | 0:55-1:06 | fold | Then the shutter opened. | The shutter turns on its hinge, revealing the night and the moon. This is the signature fold. |
| 7 | 1:06-1:18 | reveal | The moon had been at the window the whole time. | The camera pulls back; the moon is large and quiet; the lamp is now small at the edge. |
| 8 | 1:18-1:28 | hold | You are allowed to leave the room. | She lifts, crosses the sill, and the frame holds on the empty room and the open window. |

**Last line.** *You are allowed to leave the room.* The only second-person line; an invitation, not
a command.

**Notes.**
- The lamp must never look evil. It is simply a lamp. The cruelty is that it does not change.
- The moon must never glow. It is a pale printed card with a cool light.
- The wing damage is cumulative and quiet: one frayed edge per circle, not a big burn.
- The shutter fold is the visual turn; give it 0.5 s of real hinge motion and a shadow sweep.

**What it is not.** Not "leave toxic people". Not a lecture about self-worth. The story says none of
those words.
